# HOLOS Intro Theme — Version 1

Project: HOLOS — The Moon Has Anger Issues
Project author: Khaled AL-MAHDY
Status: Creative draft / production handoff; not approved final audio
Target duration: approximately 54 seconds

## Objective
Create a memorable series-identification theme. Begin with approximately five seconds of epic space-opera fanfare, then cut sharply into quirky, energetic alternative rock with a science-fiction edge. The song introduces the show's premise and character dynamics without giving characters separate spoken or sung dialogue lines. Music is intended to sit beneath a title sequence and animation montage.

## Canonical cast
- Khaled: Senior Relational Physicist; controlled, sceptical, precise, evidence-driven, dryly sarcastic. He checks the data and protects the record.
- Kevin: Junior Systems Investigator; intelligent, impulsive, funny, insecure and rebellious. He is tempted to press the dangerous button before thinking.
- ORBIT: Brutally literal station AI; calm, operationally precise and deadpan. No humanoid body is implied.
- The Moon / Lunar Interface: unresolved voice through connected lunar observation systems.
- The Unknown Voice / Fourth Signal: distinct from lunar regions A/B/C; origin and identity remain unknown.

The song is sung by an external narrator. Do not impersonate characters or turn them into vocal performers.

## Sound
Male mid-low lead, clear diction, restrained delivery, minimal vibrato, no screaming. Five-second instrumental brass/timpani/cosmic synth opening. Hard cut to punchy drums, bouncy melodic distorted bass, octave-fuzz guitar, spring reverb, subtle tape saturation, playful stop-start accents and a memorable chorus.
