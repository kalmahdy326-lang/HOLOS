# Arrangement plan — Version 1

Target duration: approximately 54 seconds
Suggested rock tempo: 168 BPM, 4/4
Project author: Khaled AL-MAHDY

| Time | Section | Arrangement |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Space-opera cold open | Brass, timpani, rising cosmic synth; no vocals |
| 0:05–0:16 | Verse | Hard cut to drums, bass, octave-fuzz guitar; lead enters |
| 0:16–0:22 | Pre-chorus | Tightening drums, rising synth, rhythmic vocal hook |
| 0:22–0:34 | Chorus | Full band, open guitars, memorable HOLOS hook |
| 0:34–0:40 | Instrumental break | Bass/guitar/synth feature and drum fill |
| 0:40–0:51 | Final chorus | Full band; build to final vocal tag |
| 0:51–0:54 | End tag/title | Anonymous processed “Look between”, title impact, clean stop |

Times are editorial targets, not measured audio. At 168 BPM a bar is about 1.429 seconds, so durations may need adjustment to fit complete bars and vocal phrasing.

## Harmonic direction
Suggested starting point, not mandatory: D minor pedal with rising upper orchestral sequence in the intro; D minor / F major colour in the rock section; compact four-chord loop; wider chorus voicings; decisive D-based final impact. Record actual chords only after selecting the audio take.

## Sound palette
Punchy live-feeling drums; melodic distorted bass; octave-fuzz guitar with spring reverb; restrained analog sci-fi synth; wide brass/timpani opening; male mid-low vocal with minimal vibrato.

## Mix priorities
1. Vocal intelligibility.
2. Kick/bass separation.
3. Preserve transient punch.
4. Wide intro without uncomfortable loudness jump.
5. Decisive final hit without accidental reverb truncation.

Do not lock LUFS, true-peak or sample-rate targets until the episode delivery specification is confirmed.
