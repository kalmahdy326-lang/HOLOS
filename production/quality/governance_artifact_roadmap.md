# HOLOS Episode 1 — Governance Artifact Roadmap

**Purpose.** Inventory the governance specifications already present, track the requested governance artifacts by function and population state, and define the evidence-dependent order for completing production records. This roadmap reports the inspected repository state; it does not approve assets, assign owners, or authorize production or release.

## Artifact classes and current state

“Normative” means a document defining requirements, process, or acceptance criteria. A “blank template” provides fields or an empty register for future evidence; it is not an approval or a completed assessment. A “generated report” is produced from an execution or completed evidence review and must be tied to its inputs and scope. A command/script is executable tooling, not a report or approval.

## Current blockers and evidence boundary

The inspected readiness matrix and intake identify these production blockers:

- **Canonical story authority — BLOCKED / UNKNOWN:** no approved canonical complete screenplay and ending are established. The shot plan says its source is a working copy, rejects an unverified 53-shot list as authority, and keeps the shot register empty. **The shot register stays empty until canonical screenplay approval** and explicit authority to create a breakdown are received.
- **Dialogue and audio — BLOCKED / PARTIAL:** four-turn transcript/timeline groundwork exists, but it is not a full approved episode script. Actual production/review WAV bytes are unavailable in the inspected repository, and the listed Manus/Codex review-WAV hashes conflict. No authoritative WAV selection or byte-level validation is established.
- **Transcript, acoustic timelines, and visemes — PARTIAL / BLOCKED:** handoff JSON and validator groundwork exist; their metadata does not verify missing source bytes. Internal phoneme subdivisions are described as estimates; audio-hash linkage, production rig mapping, and production-scene binding remain unvalidated.
- **Character and world assets — BLOCKED / UNKNOWN or NOT STARTED:** original Khaled/Kevin production rigs and dependencies, full production project, environments, props, cameras, and integrated episode scenes are not established in the inspected evidence. Prototypes and generic infrastructure do not replace these assets.
- **Shared clock and integration — PARTIAL / BLOCKED:** contract/scaffold and CI groundwork exist, but the actual audio, timeline, rigs, and production scene have not been shown integrated and validated together.
- **Render, review, delivery — NOT STARTED / BLOCKED:** no complete body render or verified final master is established. Bookend previews/credits and draft music are not final approvals. Delivery profile, shot-level human review, complete assembly, subtitle/credits approval, licensing review, and archive verification remain outstanding.
- **Approval and accountability — NOT ESTABLISHED:** no actual approval decisions, named reviewers, or role assignments should be inferred from illustrative examples or role labels in criteria. Record real identities only when supplied and authorized.

These are statuses of the inspected evidence set, not assertions that missing items cannot exist elsewhere. Repository CI/tooling passes and generated inventory reports support only the checks and bytes they actually inspect; they do not establish production readiness or episode completion.

## Dependency order for population

1. **Receive, preserve, and identify authoritative source inputs.** Obtain the canonical complete screenplay and ending with stable version/hash and explicit approval; record whether an approved shot authority exists or breakdown is authorized. Preserve originals. Intake exact audio bytes and resolve which review WAV is authoritative (or receive a canonical export); obtain original rigs/project/world packages and delivery/rights inputs. Record provenance without inventing names or values.
2. **Approve screenplay, then derive the shot register.** Only after canonical screenplay approval and breakdown authorization may shot candidates be derived from cited passages. Keep them unscheduled until their row-level story, duration, asset, and role/review gates pass. Until then the register remains empty.
3. **Accept linked dialogue and audio.** Approve the complete dialogue/script version against screenplay; verify exact selected audio bytes/hash, format and duration, transcript match and turn mapping. Resolve transcript/alignment linkage; regenerate or validate acoustic word/phoneme timelines and visemes against the selected exact audio and record limitations/reviews. This supplies evidence for script/audio records and acceptance checks.
4. **Accept rigs and production assets.** Inventory actual rigs and scene/project dependencies; record IDs, versions, hashes, provenance/license, validation reports and named decisions. Validate rig controls, deformation, performance, and render tests. Accept environments, props, music and other assets against their type-specific criteria.
5. **Integrate and validate the production scene.** Link the accepted WAV, timeline, verified rig mappings and one shared transport clock; run the contract tests in the actual project. Build scenes/layout/cameras and shot records only from approved screenplay-derived material and accepted assets. Record runtime logs and review evidence.
6. **Lock delivery profile, render, and review.** Approve versioned profile values before rendering. Generate candidate renders; perform automated stream/frame/audio checks and independent full visual/audio review against the exact hash. Complete shot/render records only from real candidates and decisions; revise through change control and revalidate changed outputs.
7. **Assemble and close release gates.** Verify master, subtitles/captions, title/credits, music and rights against approved source evidence; complete licensing review, final acceptance record, and release readiness evaluation for the exact commit/profile/output. Archive the actual accepted artifacts and reports, hash and verify copied bytes, then populate archive manifest and final acceptance approvals.
8. **Generate repository snapshot last or at each requested checkpoint.** Run `repository_state_audit.sh` after relevant changes to produce `repository_state_audit_report.json`. Treat it as a timestamped informational snapshot of the inspected checkout, never as approval or a replacement for evidence-level acceptance records.

No artifact in this roadmap authorizes `COMPLETE`, `REVIEWED`, or `LOCKED` for Episode 1. Those statuses require the applicable source, provenance, validation, integration, render, review, approval, and archive evidence for exact versions and hashes.
