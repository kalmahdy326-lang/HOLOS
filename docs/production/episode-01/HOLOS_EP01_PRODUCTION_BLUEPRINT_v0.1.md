## 1. Executive direction

This blueprint converts the nine supplied dramatic phases into a 65-shot production proposal, a source/asset dependency register, an audio cue plan and a structured JSON planning feed.

The supplied phase boundaries and dramatic functions are retained. The 15:14.84 runtime is retained. No new speaking roles, scenes or dialogue have been invented. Each proposed shot maps to an existing screenplay beat or to the owner-confirmed 12-second opening.

The 65 shot IDs in this blueprint are new production IDs. They are not claims that the original 65 storyboard panel IDs have been recovered or reconciled. The original panel IDs and order must be preserved when the source storyboard is loaded.

### Deliverable status

- This is a scene/shot production plan and engine-feed draft.
- It is not an approved animatic, locked storyboard, final asset manifest, render-ready Godot configuration or completed episode.
- The nine-phase pacing table supplied by the creator controls the timeline.
- The screenplay supplies scene action and dialogue, subject to source-revision comparison.

**Panel/shot count clarification (2026-11-26):** The 65 `EP01_P...` entries in this proposed production shot list are *not* the original storyboard panel IDs. The canonical storyboard contains 62 main-story panels plus three separate post-credit panels. The proposed 65-shot schedule currently assigns all 65 production-shot entries to the 15:14.84 main program; it has not yet been mapped one-to-one or many-to-one against the 62 main-story panel IDs. This is not a validated equivalence and must not be described as “65 storyboard panels in the main cut.” Some original panels may require multiple production shots, but that must be demonstrated by the panel-to-shot map and approved animatic, not assumed. The separate three post-credit panel IDs remain outside this main-program schedule. The shot durations in this draft are therefore proposed editorial allocations, not frame-accurate accepted panel durations.

**Pacing control:** this is the highest-density phase. Keep dialogue intelligible through effects. Visual hierarchy: convergence, warning, physical failure, injury.

### P07 — Controlled crisis (09:51–11:58)
43. Khaled reaches Kevin and learns about the processor.
44. Khaled orders ORBIT to cut it; isolation fails.
45. ORBIT explains active control-state integration; Khaled refuses unsupported life inference.
46. Geometry deviation grows; floor/support/console failure.

48. Khaled chooses independent control without destroying raw observations.
49. Isolation 1 — common power; signal continues.
50. Isolation 2 — shared clock; Isolation 3 — shared communications; signal persists.

**Pacing control:** three operations must be individually legible and sonically distinct:
- Common power: relay opens and lights dim.
- Shared clock: synchronization ticks stop.
- Communications: carrier drops, but the signal remains.

Do not compress the operations into one montage. Protect the long pause after the third test.

### P08 — False resolution / debrief (11:58–13:55)
51. Signal remains; Khaled says stop assuming.
52. Khaled clarifies they test necessary relations, not separation of the Moon.
53. Restore original partition; preserve raw data and disable feedback.
54. Regions separate; station stabilizes; voice fades.
55. ORBIT confirms shutdown; survival/drink/medical exchange.
56. Later damaged chamber, repair drones and Kevin's bandage.

58. Geometry alternatives remain; baseline explanation still viable.

**Pacing control:** restore station tone. No triumphant resolution cue; the mystery remains.

### P09 — Final reframe (13:55–15:14.84)
59. Baseline explanation may still win; no invariant may be found.
60. Kevin articulates the relational idea as a question; shared laugh.
61. ORBIT receives unclassified Region C packet.
62. Pre-feedback timestamp and fourth structure not matching A/B/C.
63. Khaled insists on raw-data preservation before playback.
64. Unknown Voice: “You have been looking at the Moon.”

**Pacing control:** archive clarity first, then the signal. The Unknown Voice is separate from ORBIT. Do not fill the final tail with exposition.

## 8. Audio and performance plan

See audio-cue-plan.csv.

- **Khaled:** grounded and measured; dry sarcasm; scientific caveats must remain clear.
- **Kevin:** faster and reactive; let humour come from timing; rhythm changes under danger/injury.
- **ORBIT:** concise, literal, consistent status cue.
- **Moon-associated voice:** unsettling but intelligible; signal-like, not monster-like.
- **Unknown Voice:** distinct final transmission, with silence preserved around it.

Sound design rules:
- No ominous score during P01 banter.
- P02 uses separate region ticks, a joint merge tone, metal strain and ORBIT alert.
- P03 protects silence after the first voice.
- P04 uses neutral model cues.
- P05 uses connector click, warning and synchronization cue.
- P06 uses low thump, feedback warning, gas release, impact and alarm in a clear hierarchy.
- P07 gives each isolation a unique sonic identity.
- P08 restores stable room tone and avoids a victory fanfare.
- P09 preserves raw-data command before Unknown Voice playback; stop at approved out-point.

The original theme remains preserved. Confirm its local digest and approved excerpt before new mixing. Do not use the theme as the dialogue master. Final loudness/true-peak/channel/caption standards depend on the eventual delivery platform; do not invent a universal spec.
