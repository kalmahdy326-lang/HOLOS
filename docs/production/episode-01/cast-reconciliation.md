## Evidence and interpretation: Dr. AL-MAHDY

The consolidated screenplay extraction's Scene 4 exchange provides direct contextual evidence:

> KEVIN: “Orbit, where is Khaled?”  
> ORBIT: “Dr. AL-MAHDY is conducting an independent calibration.”

Source: `docs/source_archive/2026-11-episode-1-the-moon-has-anger-issues-screenplay.extracted.md`, Scene 4, repository extraction around lines 616–620 (page 11–12 of the 25-page extraction).

Because ORBIT's answer immediately responds to Kevin asking where Khaled is, the leading interpretation is that “Dr. AL-MAHDY” is Khaled, Character B, referred to by title and surname while off-screen. The screenplay cover and creator credit also spell the project author's name “Khaled AL-MAHDY.” The secondary-source form “Dr. Elmahdi” is an error; the correct spelling is “Dr. AL-MAHDY,” matching the screenplay exchange and creator’s correction.

**Confidence/status:** Strong contextual inference, not yet an explicit creator-approved canon lock. The correct spelling is creator-confirmed as “AL-MAHDY.” The contextual reading that Dr. AL-MAHDY refers to Khaled (Character B) is strongly supported by the Scene 4 question-and-answer. Do not treat “Elmahdi” as an alternate spelling or separate identity. The screenplay uses the title “Dr.”; do not infer further biography or credentials.
