## 1. Source authority and limits

This breakdown is derived from the repository's 25-page extracted screenplay titled *Episode 1 — The Moon Has Anger Issues*. The extraction identifies its source as `HOLOS_Episode_1_The_Moon_Has_Anger_Issues_Complete_Screenplay.docx`, but also states that it is a consolidated chat-derived working copy and was not compared verbatim against the original DOCX. It must therefore be reconciled with the original file and explicitly approved before this breakdown becomes a locked production plan.

The breakdown preserves the screenplay's seven scenes and post-credit scene. It does not add dialogue, decide disputed wording, invent character designs, or treat the fictional information/geometry anomaly as established physics. Shot order and coverage are proposed editorial decisions for review.

## 2. Production rules

- **Dialogue lock:** Use only the approved screenplay and the selected authoritative audio master. Do not regenerate dialogue or infer line timing from this shot list.
- **Character lock:** Khaled and Kevin must use their original independent rigs and approved designs. The Moon and Unknown Voice are interface/audio phenomena in this script; no humanoid Moon character is specified.
- **Science integrity:** Show correlations as correlations. The story explicitly keeps baseline explanations, measurement error, extensions and radical alternatives open.
- **Frame accuracy:** The repository's 1280×720 / 24 fps values are starter settings, not confirmed delivery requirements. No frame ranges are assigned here.
- **Continuity:** Preserve geography, screen direction, hologram labels A/B/C, the Region C disconnect, Kevin's forehead injury, chamber damage, and the fourth signal's distinct visual language.
- **No fake completion:** These are proposed shots. They are not rendered assets, approved storyboards, animation, or a final edit.

## 3. Episode-wide visual and editorial direction

The visual language should begin with controlled, legible scientific observation. As the feedback pathway destabilizes the station, environmental movement and camera energy may increase, but action must remain spatially readable. The controlled test in Scene 6 should deliberately reduce visual chaos. Scene 7 returns to stillness before the fourth-signal reveal.

The lunar facility is industrial, engineered and physically credible: pressure doors, shielding, cables, control systems, observation modules and repair infrastructure. Holograms should communicate data and relationships, not magical energy. The abstract post-credit space is the one intentional departure from the physical lunar environment.

## 4. Character performance direction

- **Khaled:** Controlled, observant, skeptical and dry. He does not claim more than the evidence supports. Under pressure, his body becomes urgent while his speech remains precise.
- **Kevin:** Intelligent and curious, fast with jokes, impulsive under pressure. His Scene 4 decision must read as a consequential mistake, not inexplicable stupidity. The injury remains visible in Scenes 5 and 7.
- **Orbit:** Operationally literal. The humour comes from exact wording and timing, not expressive anthropomorphic animation.
- **The Moon:** A voice associated with the connected lunar observation interface. The source remains unknown; do not add a face or imply consciousness as a fact.
- **Unknown Voice:** Distinct from the Moon's interface voice unless the project author later specifies otherwise. Keep its source unresolved.
